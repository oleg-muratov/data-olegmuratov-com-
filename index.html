London Airbnb — How Posted Prices Behave Over the Calendar

Listing fixed effects · two-way clustered standard errors · 24M listing-days · Python (pandas, pyfixest, DuckDB)

How do 85,000 London Airbnb listings price the year ahead? About a third post one price on every date. Within the same listing, posted prices are 2.4% higher on Friday and Saturday nights and about 11% higher over Christmas and New Year, and professional hosts' listings show roughly twice the premium. Built on posted prices only, with no booking or revenue claims the data can't support.
